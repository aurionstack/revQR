Product features

A complete Google review QR code workflow

From the first scan to Google Business Profile, revQR removes unnecessary steps while keeping every review grounded in the customer’s own experience.

Create your branded QR code

01 · QR design

Branded QR codes that are ready to display

Add your business name, logo, colors, and an optional table, counter, staff, or location tag. Download the QR code alone or export the entire high-resolution standee design.

02 · Customer input

Multiple highlights and notes shape the draft

Customers choose more than one relevant highlight and can add their own details. Those inputs guide the draft, making it more specific than a generic review template.

03 · AI assistance

Editable review drafts that match the rating

The assistant uses the selected rating, tags, notes, and business context to draft natural wording. Customers can edit or regenerate before choosing what to copy.

04 · Google destination

Direct handoff to the connected review section

After copying their draft, customers continue to the review section of the Google Business Profile link configured by the business owner.

05 · Analytics

Understand scans, ratings, and source performance

See scan volume, completed review drafts, rating distribution, and source-tag performance so you know which placements receive engagement.

06 · Service recovery

Give every customer a private feedback option

Customers can send a private note to the business instead of publishing it. Every rating follows the same transparent flow—revQR does not hide the Google option based on sentiment.
