Common questions

Google review QR code FAQ

What does a Google review QR code do?

It gives customers a scannable path from your physical location to a short feedback flow and then to the review section of your connected Google Business Profile.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. revQR runs in the phone browser, so customers can scan with their camera and complete the flow without downloading anything.

Does revQR post reviews automatically?

No. revQR creates an editable draft from the customer’s selected rating, highlights, and notes. The customer chooses what to copy and personally submits it to Google.

Can I use different QR codes for tables or locations?

Yes. Add an optional table, counter, staff, or location tag to measure where scans and completed review drafts originate.

Can I download the full QR standee design?

Yes. You can customize and download an exact 4 × 6 inch, 1200 × 1800 px standee image. Optional physical stand ordering is coming soon.
