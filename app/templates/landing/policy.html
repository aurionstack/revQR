A simpler way to hear from your customers

RevQR is a QR-based customer feedback and review-assistance service operated under the {{ config.LEGAL_BUSINESS_NAME }} trading brand, based in Mapusa, Goa, India. We help local businesses make it easier for customers to share their real experiences.

What we do

Businesses can connect their Google review link, personalise QR artwork with their branding, download a standee image and track feedback from tables, counters or other locations. Customers scan the code, choose a rating and share highlights or notes. Optional AI assistance turns those details into an editable draft; customers decide what to copy and post themselves.

The business dashboard includes review history, source analytics, manual reply drafting and AI-assisted replies. Physical QR stands are an optional additional purchase when ordering is available.

Real experiences, not manufactured reviews

We do not sell reviews or promise search rankings. All ratings have the same option to post on Google. AI drafts must be checked by the customer and must not invent experiences.

Clear plans and human support

Subscriptions are ₹1,599 for one year or ₹2,499 for two years, with manual renewal. Physical stands are listed at ₹249 per item when ordering is open. See our pricing, fair-use terms, refunds and shipping information before purchasing.

RevQR is independent of Google and Razorpay; these services control their own accounts, payment processing and review publication. Aurion Stack is our trading brand, not a claim of company incorporation.

{% elif page == 'privacy' %}

Information we use

Business names, login emails, password hashes, profile links, branding, subscription records and uploaded logos help us operate your account. We store customer ratings, notes, editable review drafts, timestamps, scan sources, user-agent data and keyed IP hashes for analytics and abuse prevention. Legacy private-feedback records submitted before that feature was retired may remain subject to our normal retention and deletion process.

Service providers

Customer notes, selected highlights and business context are sent to Google Gemini for drafting. Avoid sensitive personal information in hints. Razorpay processes payments; RevQR stores order/payment references and statuses, not full card data. Hosting/database providers store application data. Email providers deliver verification and purchase messages. Delivery details are shared with the courier for physical orders.

Cookies and retention

We use necessary cookies for sessions and verification flows. Operational logs and transactional records support security, fraud prevention and fulfilment. We retain account data while needed for the service; contact support to request access, correction or deletion. Some payment records may need retention for accounting or legal obligations. We do not promise immediate deletion from existing backups.

Your choices

Customer reviews remain editable and are posted only by the customer. Ask support about access, correction, deletion and provider processing. We do not sell customer review data.

The service

RevQR provides branded QR artwork, business analytics, AI-assisted review drafts and optional physical stands. It is not affiliated with Google or Razorpay. We do not guarantee Google rankings, review publication, a particular number of reviews or uninterrupted third-party services.

Honest reviews only

Use genuine experiences, not fabricated reviews, impersonation, incentives or rating manipulation. Every star rating has the same Google posting option. You must check AI drafts before use; business background must not become invented customer experiences.

Subscriptions and fair use

One business account costs ₹1,599 for 12 months or ₹2,499 for 24 months. The two-year option saves ₹699 (approximately 22%) against two annual purchases. Renewals are manual, not automatic recurring charges. Owner replies share the same business allowance. Requests may count even if the provider fails. Provider outages, global safety budgets and abuse controls may limit AI availability; manual editing/posting remains available.

Payments and responsibilities

Prices shown at checkout are the final amount charged for that purchase. A payment receipt is not a GST tax invoice. Keep your login secure, verify your connected Google listing, and upload only assets you have permission to use. You retain ownership of your submitted content. Access may be suspended for abuse or fraud after review.

Physical stands

Cancel before custom production begins for a full refund. Once production has started, personalised stands are not ordinarily refundable for change of mind.
